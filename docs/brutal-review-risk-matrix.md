### Severity rules (applied above)
- **Critical:** Can produce a wrong business decision, count unproven vendor invoice amount, expose arbitrary files, silently lose audit/history, corrupt bundle status, or crash the main flow. (#1, #2, #3, #4, #5, #6, #7, #8.)
- **High:** Stale UI/export disagreement, extraction-mode ambiguity, manual-patch bypass risk, OCR/model unsafe behavior, missing validation around uploaded files, or operational/non-reproducible-build risk. (#9 through #21.)
- **Medium:** Poor UX/error messaging, oversized service, duplicated logic, missing test coverage, docs mismatch. (#22 through #51.)
- **Low:** Naming, minor polish, small tech debt. (#52 through #62.)
